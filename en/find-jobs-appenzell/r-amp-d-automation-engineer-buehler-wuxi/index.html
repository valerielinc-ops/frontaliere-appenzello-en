Role overview

In 2020, Bühler Premier Tech (Wuxi) Packaging Machinery Co., Ltd. is a joint venture between the Swiss Bühler Group and the Canadian Premier Group (50% each), relying on the superior resources of Bühler and Premier, integrating their product lines and professional capabilities to make future packaging solutions more efficient through the application of automation technology, bringing the industry more cost-effective and safer semi-automatic/automatic packaging solutions.

Aufgaben

  • Work on design, develop, and maintain PLC programs for automatic machines, robot palletizer
  • Partly work on the electrical wire diagram for standard products, as well as customer orders.
  • Partly support the startup team at shopfloor to perform system testing, debugging, and commissioning at customer by request
  • Collaborate with mechanical, pneumatic, and startup team for system integration, validation
  • Prepare technical documentation, including functional descriptions, manuals, and reports
  • Ensure version control of software and electrical deliverable.
  • During Roadmap development, work closely with the design authority (overseas) to develop / transfer new products or new functions
  • During Life cycle management, optimize the products, localize the design
  • For customization, work closely with project team to develop customized solutions to match customer needs.
