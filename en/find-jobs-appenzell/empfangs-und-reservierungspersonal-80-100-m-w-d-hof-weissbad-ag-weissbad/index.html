Full description

Starting immediately or by arrangement We are looking for people with passion. For their profession, for the team, and for our guests. As an innovative and successful company, we offer attractive employment conditions in one of the most beautiful resorts in Eastern Switzerland. We owe our success to our approximately 270 friendly and motivated employees with great team spirit. These are your tasks

  • You are the warm and courteous contact person at the reception – check-ins and check-outs
  • You take care of guest services on site - information, advice, sales
  • You perform additional tasks - vouchers, kiosk, table reservations, general administrative work
  • You manage room contingents internally & externally
  • You handle general correspondence, such as offers, confirmations, invoices, etc.
  • You answer incoming calls and operate the telephone switchboard
  • You manage contingents on external reservation systems

What we expect from you

  • You have training in the hotel or tourism sector or comparable professional experience at a 4*-hotel reception
  • You love guest contact, are communicative and always remain charming
  • You have a strong service orientation and are always ready to go the extra mile for our guests
  • You know the region or are willing to acquire this knowledge independently
  • You are reliable, extremely conscientious and enjoy working independently as well as in a team
  • You speak German and English – additional languages are a plus
  • You are IT-savvy and familiar with common hotel systems (Opera Cloud, Aleno, common Office programmes)

What we offer you

  • Family-friendly : we offer you 5 weeks of holiday, the possibility of part-time work
  • Catering : our kitchen also conjures up fresh, healthy and subsidised meals for our employees
  • Health : to stay fit and healthy, our employees use the bathing and fitness area free of charge
  • Support : we promote your professional development through a contribution to further training costs
  • Discounts : you benefit from discounts in our hotel, in the region as well as with partner companies of the ERFA group and the Private Selection Hotels
  • Additional benefits : weekend allowances and free parking
