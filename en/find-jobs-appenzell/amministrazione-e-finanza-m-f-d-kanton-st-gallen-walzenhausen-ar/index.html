Full description

The Migration Office operates daily in the exciting political environment of all migration issues. We are responsible for the permits under foreigners law, enforce the Swiss alien and asylum law, issue the biometric Swiss passports and foreigner ID cards and provide care and integration in our asylum centres.

What to expect

  • You lead the administration of the center and manage the dossiers of asylum seekers
  • You create lists, statistics and billing and take on a variety of administrative tasks
  • You organize entry and exit and support in social and health administrative processes
  • You manage the telephone exchange and counter service and process the incoming and outgoing mail
  • You coordinate appointments and consultation hours, are in contact with municipalities, authorities and advisory centres and process the correspondence
  • You organize meetings and keep minutes
  • You take on tasks in the financial area, especially in the accounts payable, cash management as well as in bills and settlements

What distinguishes you

  • You have a completed commercial training EFZ and ideally bring your first professional experience in the administrative environment
  • You like to work organized, keep an overview even in a varied work day and complete tasks reliably and carefully
  • Contact with people gives you pleasure and you meet different stakeholders in an appreciative, open and service-oriented way.
  • You have good IT user knowledge and can quickly find your way around in new programs
  • You work independently, think and actively participate as a committed team member
  • Foreign language skills and a category B driving licence are beneficial

What we offer

  • An exciting and varied activity in the field of asylum
  • A dedicated and interdisciplinary team with appreciative cooperation
  • Insight into different cultures and languages
  • Various training and further education opportunities as well as attractive social benefits of the Canton of St.Gallen
  • Pensum: 80-100 %
  • Location: Walzenhausen/AR
  • Application via the official job portal of the Canton of St. Gallen (https://www.sg.ch/ueber-den-kanton-st-gallen/employer-canton-stgallen/job portal.html)
