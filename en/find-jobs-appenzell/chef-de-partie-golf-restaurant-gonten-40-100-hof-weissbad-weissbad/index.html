Chef de Partie - Golf Restaurant Gonten 100 % (m | f | d) Immediate start or by arrangement

From 2026, Resort Hof Weissbad will take over the gastronomy of the Golf Restaurant Gonten, creating a new culinary experience for everyone. Whether after a round of golf, for a spontaneous visit, family celebrations, or corporate events: the publicly accessible restaurant will in future offer the culinary signature of Resort Hof Weissbad.

Become part of our kitchen team. You can expect an exciting project in the successful, family-run, and growth-oriented Resort Hof Weissbad, with many opportunities to realize your ideas. These are your tasks

  • You work independently in a varied kitchen
  • You create contemporary, creative, and high-quality dishes
  • You help ensure smooth workflows
  • You comply with quality and hygiene guidelines and carry out quality checks of goods

What we expect from you

  • You have training as a cook EFZ
  • You have already gained professional experience in a similar position
  • You have excellent expertise in your field
  • You are an organizational talent
  • You are flexible and can be deployed in all kitchen stations
  • Your commitment and flexibility distinguish you
  • You are creative and have high quality and hygiene standards

What we offer you

  • Meals : our kitchen also conjures up fresh, healthy, and subsidized meals for our employees
  • Health : to stay fit and healthy, our employees use the bathing and fitness area free of charge
  • Employee accommodation : affordable employee accommodations available in the area
  • Support : we promote your professional development through cost-sharing for further training
  • Discounts : you benefit from discounts in our hotel, in the region, as well as with partner companies of the ERFA group and the Private Selection Hotels
  • Additional benefits : weekend bonuses and free parking

Sounds like your dream job? Then get to know us – we look forward to people who are passionate hosts with us .
