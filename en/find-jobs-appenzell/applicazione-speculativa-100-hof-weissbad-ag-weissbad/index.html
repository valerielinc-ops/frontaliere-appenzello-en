Role overview

From April 2024 or by arrangement, we are looking for a

Specialist FMH as Head of Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation (60–100%, m/f/d)

You have an FMH specialist title in Orthopedic Surgery and Traumatology of the Musculoskeletal System or Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation or Rheumatology as well as at least three years of experience in musculoskeletal rehabilitation.

  • From April 2024 or by arrangement, we are looking for a
  • Specialist FMH as Head of Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation (60–100%, m/f/d)

Application process

  • Our holistic treatment concept requires understanding and openness to recognized complementary medical applications.
  • You take over the medical management of the musculoskeletal rehabilitation and spa department.
  • Your main tasks include the independent care of inpatient rehab and spa patients as well as determining the treatment steps.
  • You conduct rounds and interdisciplinary reports and are the first point of contact for patients as well as for the staff of the practice, nursing, and therapy teams.
  • You work closely with professionals from various departments and ensure high treatment quality.
  • Seamless and meaningful documentation as well as recording of medical services is a matter of course for you.
  • You are responsible for on-call and weekend duty and participate in it.
  • In addition to working on the ward, you provide outpatient medical services in your own consultation hours; for guests of Hof Weissbad AG as well as for external patients.
