Full description

Your responsibilities

  • Assembly and testing of high-precision micro-parts according to work instructions
  • Production of micro-connectors with the highest quality requirements
  • Operation and monitoring of semi-automatic systems in the microtechnology sector
  • Carrying out final microscopic tests and professional packaging of products
  • Feedback of production orders in the SAP system
  • Booking of individual parts on production orders in the SAP system
  • Ensuring the required quality standards and compliance with all relevant regulations
  • Application and participation in the implementation of lean methods and continuous improvements
  • Working in a clean room environment according to defined quality and process standards

Your profile

  • Technical or mechanical basic training or corresponding further development desirable
  • Experience in the assembly and processing of micro-parts as well as in the use of optical aids
  • Practice in the operation and monitoring of machines and plants, ideally with CNC-controlled systems
  • Good technical understanding of production processes, equipment and semi-automated plants
  • Safe handling of technical work instructions, specifications and documentation
  • Very good eyesight, pronounced concentration and high fine motor skills
  • Precise, quality-conscious and reliable work method
  • Willingness to actively shape new processes and bring in optimization potential
  • Very good German skills in word and writing, English skills are advantageous
  • Flexibility and readiness for overtime and shift operation (3-shift)
