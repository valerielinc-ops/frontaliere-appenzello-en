Role overview

Sputtering Components Inc. (SCI) in Owatonna is a business in southern Minnesota that has served customers all around the world for over 20 years.

As a CNC Machinist, you'll take ownership of complex machining assignments, working independently to produce high-quality components while ensuring production goals are met efficiently and safely.
