Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Appenzell - a successful, locally anchored bank - we are looking for an entrepreneurial and customer-oriented personality as Head of Corporate Client Consulting (m/f/d). Help us to further expand our position as a preferred bank for companies.

What awaits you?

  • Leadership, promotion and development of the corporate client team with a positive, appreciative and motivating leadership style
  • Professional, sales and personnel management as well as further development of corporate customer business
  • Ensuring comprehensive and needs-based advice to our corporate clients
  • Creation of individual solution proposals for the entire Raiffeisen Group consulting and service offering in the corporate customer business, taking into account the specific customer needs
  • Maintaining and expanding existing customer relationships as well as targeted acquisition of new customers based on defined market development
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market processing measures and performance of representative tasks in the region

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training, ideally with further training in banking or finance (e.g. business administration, corporate finance)
  • Ideally first management experience as well as the ability to motivate, promote and develop employees
  • Team-capable and entrepreneurial personality with a pronounced customer orientation, negotiating skills and a secure appearance
  • Solution-oriented, independent and networked working method
  • Several years of support and acquisition experience, especially in the SME segment

With commitment and expertise, we are committed to our customers every day. Become part of our team and bring your strengths to an inspiring work environment. We look forward to meeting you. Manuela Rutz-Büchler Head of Sales Why Raiffeisen?

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.
