Full description

Your responsibilities

  • Responsibility for autonomous operation and support of cutting and bending equipment
  • Ensuring a smooth, efficient and high-quality production process
  • Flexible use as a jumper between cutting to length/bending and assembly
  • Precise assembly of miniature connectors with increased quality requirements
  • Independent reading and transfer of working papers and technical drawings
  • Provision of materials, working documents, tools and devices
  • Processing and distribution of supplied materials
  • Implementation of material inventories
  • Carrying out tests according to specifications as well as final tests, QA approval and packaging work
  • Recording and feedback of production orders in SAP

Your profile

  • Completed technical training e.g. as an automatist, polymechanic, production mechanic or in a comparable profession
  • Professional experience in a similar capacity
  • Experience in an industrial production environment
  • High independence, reliability and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Precise, structured and quality-oriented working methods
  • Resilient and resilient personality with a positive attitude and team spirit
  • Communication strength and enjoyment of cross-sectoral cooperation
  • SAP basic knowledge is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • English skills are a plus
  • Flexibility and willingness to work overtime and shifts
