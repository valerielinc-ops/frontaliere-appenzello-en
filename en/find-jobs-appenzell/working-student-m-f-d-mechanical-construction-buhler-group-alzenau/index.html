Role overview

Bühler Leybold Optics stands for innovative technologies and steady growth.

As part of the Bühler Group, a global engineering group with over 13,000 employees, we are one of the market leaders in the field of high-tech thin film vacuum coating systems.

For more than 150 years, we have been offering our customers a wide range of systems for the coating of spectacle lenses, optical components in smartphones, cars, medical technology cameras, car head-up displays and much more.
