Full description

By arrangement The 4-star-plus Resort Hof Weissbad enjoys an excellent reputation as an innovative and successful company. In one of the most beautiful resorts in Eastern Switzerland, you will find not only a warm team spirit but also attractive employment conditions. We owe our success to our approximately 270 friendly and motivated employees. These are your tasks

  • You are responsible for cleaning your assigned floor
  • You support the co-management of housekeeping and take over daily responsibility if needed
  • You actively participate in cleaning guest rooms and public areas and can be deployed in all shifts without difficulty
  • You maintain close contact with various departments and assist them as needed
  • You are responsible for compliance with hygiene standards in your area
  • You ensure the preservation of materials through proper use

What we expect from you

  • You have a qualification as a hotel specialist EFZ or hotel housekeeping specialist EFZ
  • You have already gained professional experience in the hotel industry
  • You are preferably familiar with Opera Cloud and Samfex
  • You have a keen sense for cleanliness and high quality standards
  • You are flexible and irregular shifts, including weekends, are natural for you
  • You can communicate well in German

What we offer you

  • Catering : our kitchen also conjures up fresh, healthy, and subsidised meals for our employees
  • Health : to stay fit and healthy, our employees use the bathing and fitness area free of charge
  • Employee accommodation : affordable employee accommodation available in the area
  • Support : we promote your professional development through cost-sharing for further training
  • Discounts : you benefit from discounts at our hotel, in the region, and with partner companies of the ERFA group and the Private Selection Hotels
  • Additional benefits : weekend allowances and free parking

We offer you an interesting position in an extraordinary hotel. Learn more about us at www.hofweissbad.ch.
