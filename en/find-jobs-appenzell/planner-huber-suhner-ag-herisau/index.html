Full description

Your responsibilities

  • Analysis and implementation of the demand planning of our key customers. Your contribution is crucial to our corporate success.
  • Assuming operational order processing and managing production planning for our diverse customer orders – internally and externally and taking into account capacities, material availability and delivery dates.
  • Maintenance of master data in the ERP system and creation of evaluations.
  • Responsibility for material management for external assemblies and ensuring smooth operation.
  • Ensure and continuously optimize key performance indicators such as throughput times, supply and stock levels.
  • Perceiving an important communicative hub function between sales, product management, logistics, purchasing, R&D and production.
  • Ensure that all relevant indicators are achieved and actively promote improvement measures.

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training in the technical or commercial sector, ideally with further training in production planning or supply chain management
  • Several years of profound professional experience in production planning and control
  • Very good knowledge of the procurement and manufacturing processes as well as the relevant IT systems (e.g. SAP PP, SAP APO, S/4HANA, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • Networked thinking, goal-oriented work and strong communication skills
  • Flexibility, resilience, team spirit and initiative
  • Strong assertiveness coupled with diplomatic skill and calm demeanor – even in challenging situations, you will keep an overview and find constructive solutions when dealing with different departments
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
