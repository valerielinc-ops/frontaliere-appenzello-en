Full description

Do you value a solid basic education? Are you an upbeat and enthusiastic personality who has good manners, an interest in banking and enjoys customer contact? Then you might soon be our new team member.

Tasks

During your three-year training, you will gain insight into various departments and will be deployed in the following work situations:

  • Receiving and referring bank customers
  • Advise bank customers
  • Support bank customers via digital channels
  • Assist in advising customers
  • Prepare and process banking transactions administratively

In addition to the classic work situations in banking, you will also encounter the following topics with us:

  • Marketing

-HR

  • Project management

In the various departments you will be accompanied and supported by competent and experienced practical trainers. You can complete your training with us either in the EFZ extended basic education or with a vocational baccalaureate.

Requirements

School-related

  • Very good knowledge of German, spoken and written
  • Requirements for learning two foreign languages
  • With vocational baccalaureate: Very good performance at the highest school level, passed the entrance examination

Personal

  • Sociable and communicative
  • Enjoy learning
  • Teamwork ability
  • Reliability and precise work
  • Open to new things

Interests

  • Enjoy everyday banking and customer contact
  • On trends and developments in the professional field and in the industry
  • Application of information sources and use of new technologies
