Full description

Your responsibilities

  • Independent setup, operation and monitoring of the thermoplastic extrusion equipment for the production of high-frequency cables
  • Equipment and materials according to production order, recipes and work instructions
  • Ensure a smooth production process and initiate necessary process adjustments
  • Carrying out, documenting and evaluating the quality checks according to the test plan
  • Preparation of plants for shift change and subsequent production orders
  • Performing autonomous maintenance and cleaning work according to specifications
  • Active participation in the continuous optimization of processes, processes and standards
  • Further development and optimization of workplace and production according to lean principles

Your profile

  • Training as plant manager EFZ, plastics technologist or other basic mechanical training
  • Professional experience in the operation of production plants, preferably in the extrusion environment
  • Technical flair and a committed and responsible way of working
  • High quality awareness and quick comprehension
  • Reliable, independent and precise working methods
  • Enjoyment of working together in a team
  • Readiness to work in three shift operation and, if necessary, in four shift operation
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
