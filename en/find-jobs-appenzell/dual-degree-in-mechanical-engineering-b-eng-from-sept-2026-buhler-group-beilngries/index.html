Role overview

At the Beilngries site, around 360 employees in the Grain Quality and Supply division are dedicated to plant and mechanical engineering in the fields of grain processing and malting and brewing.

This includes cleaning, drying, transport (including handling technology and ship loading), storage and digitalization to process grain from the field to the consumer.

Always with a focus on efficiency, product quality and sustainability.
